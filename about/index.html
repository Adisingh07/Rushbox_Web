Small games. Smooth moments. One box full of fun.

Rush Box is a growing collection of colorful, easy-to-pick-up games made for quick sessions, satisfying gameplay and those moments when you simply want to play something fun.

About Rush Box

Rush Box brings different kinds of casual games together in one simple place. Instead of focusing on a single genre, Rush Box is built around a collection of games that can grow over time — from thoughtful puzzle challenges to classic strategy and fast arcade action.

Every game has its own identity, gameplay style and visual experience while sharing the same goal: responsive controls, smooth animations, clear feedback and an enjoyable experience across supported devices.

Whether you have a few minutes to solve a puzzle, want to challenge an AI, or simply want to beat your previous score, Rush Box is designed to make starting a game quick and getting back to it even easier.

What's Inside

➡️

Arrow Tap Out

A strategic tile-clearing puzzle where every move matters. Tap an arrow tile and it moves in the direction it points when its path is clear. Remove tiles in the right sequence, open blocked paths and clear the entire board.

⭕

Tic Tac Toe

The classic 3×3 game with a modern experience. Play against another player, challenge the AI across different difficulty levels, or enter Rush Mode for a faster challenge with smooth turn and result feedback.

🍉

Fruit Slice

Swipe through flying fruits, build combos and protect your hearts while avoiding bombs. Take on structured levels or test your reflexes and score in Rush Mode.
