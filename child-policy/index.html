Child Privacy Policy

This Child Privacy Policy explains how Rush Box approaches children's privacy and the use of its games by children.

Effective Date: [Insert Date]   •   Last Updated: [Insert Date]

1. Our Commitment to Children's Privacy

Rush Box is committed to protecting the privacy of children and providing a safe, age-appropriate gaming experience. We do not intentionally ask children to provide personal information to play the core Rush Box games.

The current Rush Box collection includes Arrow Tap Out, Tic Tac Toe, and Fruit Slice.

2. No Account Required

The core Rush Box games currently do not require a child to create an account, provide an email address, provide a phone number, or submit a real name in order to play.

We do not intentionally require children to create social profiles or provide personal contact information to access the core gameplay experience.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect From Children

We do not intentionally request or collect the following information directly from children through the core gameplay experience:

  • Full name
  • Home or physical address
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Passwords
  • Government identification information
  • Financial or payment information
  • Contacts or address books
  • Private messages
  • Photos, videos, microphone recordings, or camera content

4. Gameplay Data

Rush Box may store certain gameplay information locally on the device, such as level progress, scores, game settings, game-specific coins, power-up inventory, unlocked items, and other gameplay preferences.

This information is used to provide the game's functionality and maintain progress. It is not intended to identify a child personally.

5. Analytics

Rush Box uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand general application and gameplay usage and to improve the games.

Depending on the device, operating system, configuration, and applicable settings, analytics services may process technical and usage information such as app events, session information, device or application information, operating system information, and pseudonymous identifiers.

We do not intentionally configure Rush Box to collect a child's name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information as part of normal gameplay analytics.

6. Advertising

Rush Box may use Google AdMob and AdMob Mediation, including Meta Audience Network as a mediation demand source, to support advertising within the application.

Advertising services may process technical information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, and other information necessary for advertising delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention, subject to the applicable configuration and legal requirements.

Important for Parents and Guardians

Advertising settings, consent mechanisms, age-related settings, and the application's target-audience configuration should be configured according to the applicable laws and the requirements of Google Play, Apple App Store, Google AdMob, Firebase, Meta, and other applicable services before release.

7. Personalized Advertising and Consent

Where applicable law requires parental consent or limits personalized advertising for children, Rush Box should use the appropriate advertising and consent settings supported by the advertising services.

Advertising may be limited to non-personalized or otherwise appropriate advertising where required. The availability and behavior of advertisements can vary by region, device, advertising inventory, and applicable privacy settings.

8. Purchases and Game Items

Rush Box may contain in-game shops or game-related items. The current games may include virtual coins, power-ups, cosmetic items, backgrounds, or other gameplay features.

Any real-money purchase functionality, if introduced, should be handled through the applicable app-store purchase system and subject to the platform's requirements. Children should not make purchases without the permission of a parent or guardian.

9. Third-Party Services

Rush Box uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — application analytics.
  • Google AdMob — advertising services.
  • Meta Audience Network — advertising demand through AdMob Mediation.

Third-party providers may change their SDKs, policies, and data-processing practices. Rush Box will update its privacy documentation when material changes require an update.

10. Parental Rights and Requests

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information to Rush Box, or if you have questions about information associated with a child's use of the application, please contact us.

Depending on applicable law, parents or guardians may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of applicable personal information.

11. Data Deletion

Much of the game's gameplay information may be stored locally on the device. Parents or guardians can generally remove local game data by deleting the application or clearing its application data, where supported by the device. This may permanently remove game progress.

Requests concerning information processed by third-party analytics or advertising providers may also be subject to those providers' procedures and applicable laws.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Rush Box. However, no application or electronic system can guarantee complete security.

13. Changes to This Child Privacy Policy

We may update this Child Privacy Policy when new games, features, analytics, advertising services, legal requirements, or other material changes are introduced. The Last Updated date will be changed when the policy is updated.
