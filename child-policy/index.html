Family & Child Safety

Child Privacy Policy

Rush Box is committed to providing a safe, delightful, and wholesome arcade gaming experience that protects the privacy of children and families.

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Family & Child Safety

Rush Box is designed to provide family-friendly, wholesome, and accessible casual arcade games for players of all ages. We take child privacy and online safety very seriously and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K (General Data Protection Regulation for Children), and Google Play Families Policies.

2. Zero Personal Data Collected From Children

Children can play all core games freely without ever providing personal identification:

❌ No Account Required Young players can jump straight into gameplay in Guest mode with zero registration or email verification required.
❌ No Personal Contact Information We never solicit or collect real names, home addresses, phone numbers, school details, or photos from children.
❌ No Device Sensor Access No microphone, camera, contacts, or personal media files are ever accessed or requested.
❌ No Social Chat Rooms There are no open public chat rooms, direct messaging channels, or unmoderated player-to-player interactions.

3. Child-Safe Advertising Standards

Where advertisements are shown, we adhere strictly to child-safe advertising requirements:

  • No Behavioral Tracking: No behavioral or interest-based ad tracking is performed on children.
  • Family-Safe Filtering: Ad content is strictly filtered to ensure age-appropriate, family-safe material.
  • Optional Rewarded Ads: Rewarded video ads are strictly optional and clearly distinguished from gameplay.

4. Parental Rights & Data Removal

Parents or legal guardians have complete authority over their family's devices and gameplay data:

  • Local Reset: You can reset all locally saved scores and unlocks at any time via Settings → "Reset All Game Progress" or by clearing the application cache.
  • Prompt Deletion: If you believe personal information of a child has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately at contact@rushbox.in or contact@connectpi.in, and we will promptly delete it within 48 hours.
