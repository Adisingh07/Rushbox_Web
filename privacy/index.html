Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Rush Box handles information when you use the Rush Box application and its games.

Effective Date: [30/08/2026]   •   Last Updated: [30/08/2026]

1. About Rush Box

Rush Box is a collection of casual and arcade-style games designed for entertainment. The current collection includes Arrow Tap Out, Tic Tac Toe, and Fruit Slice.

Rush Box may be expanded in the future with additional games and features. If future features materially change how information is collected or used, this Privacy Policy may be updated as necessary.

2. Information We Collect

Rush Box does not currently require you to create an account to play the core games. Depending on how you use the application and the services enabled in your region, certain information may be processed.

2.1 Gameplay and Usage Information

We may process information about how the application is used, including:

  • Games opened or played
  • Levels started, completed, or failed
  • Game modes selected
  • Game sessions and gameplay events
  • Scores and high scores
  • Tutorial and feature interactions
  • Power-up and shop interactions
  • General application interaction and performance events

This information is primarily used to understand how the games are used and to improve gameplay, performance, stability, and future updates.

3. Firebase Analytics

Rush Box uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase Analytics) to understand application usage and improve the games.

Depending on the device, operating system, configuration, and enabled features, Firebase Analytics may process information such as:

  • App usage and interaction events
  • Session information
  • Device and application information
  • Approximate location information where applicable
  • App version and operating system information
  • Language or other automatically available properties
  • Gameplay events configured by us
  • Pseudonymous application or device identifiers

We use Firebase Analytics to:

  • Understand game usage and feature engagement
  • Improve game design and balance
  • Measure feature performance
  • Identify usage trends
  • Improve application quality and performance

We do not intentionally use Firebase Analytics to collect passwords, payment card numbers, private communications, or other information that is unnecessary for analytics. We also do not intentionally send directly identifying information such as an email address as an Analytics user identifier.

4. Advertising

Rush Box may display advertisements to support the development and maintenance of the application. We use Google AdMob and AdMob Mediation, which may allow third-party advertising partners, including Meta Audience Network, to participate in serving advertisements.

Depending on your location, device, consent choices, and applicable requirements, advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, app and ad interactions, ad impressions, ad requests, performance information, and information needed for fraud prevention, measurement, attribution, and advertising delivery.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable law, your consent or privacy choices, and the configuration of the advertising services.

5. Meta Audience Network

Meta Audience Network may be used as an advertising demand source through AdMob Mediation. When Meta participates in serving an advertisement, Meta may process information according to its own privacy practices and applicable requirements.

Where legally required, appropriate consent and privacy choices will be provided before personalized advertising or other regulated processing takes place.

6. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Privacy requirements differ depending on where you live. For users in certain regions, including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, applicable laws may require consent before certain forms of advertising-related processing occur.

Where required, Rush Box may provide a consent or privacy-choice interface. Your choices may affect personalized advertising, non-personalized advertising, analytics, and certain advertising identifiers or signals.

Consent choices may also need to be communicated to participating advertising partners through the applicable advertising SDKs and mediation systems.

7. Game-Specific Information

Arrow Tap Out

Arrow Tap Out may store gameplay information such as level progress, completed levels, scores, game-specific coins, power-up inventory, and gameplay preferences. Analytics may record gameplay events such as level starts, completions, failures, and feature interactions.

Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe may store game mode selections, player-entered names where applicable, scores, match results, difficulty selections, and local gameplay history where implemented. Player names should not contain sensitive or highly personal information.

Fruit Slice

Fruit Slice may store level progress, scores, best scores, game-specific coins, power-up inventory, purchased or unlocked themes, slice effects, and game preferences. Gameplay events may also be processed through Analytics to understand feature usage.

8. Local Game Data

Certain gameplay information may be stored locally on your device, including progress, settings, scores, best scores, unlocks, game-specific coins, power-up inventory, cosmetic items, and other gameplay preferences.

Locally stored data may remain on your device until you delete the application, clear its data, use an available reset feature, or the data is otherwise removed. Deleting the application or clearing its data may permanently remove local progress.

9. No Account or Social Profile Required

Rush Box currently does not require an account or social profile to play the core games. We do not require your real name, home address, phone number, social media account, or email address to access the core gameplay experience.

If future versions introduce accounts, cloud saves, online multiplayer, social features, or other account-based functionality, this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary.

10. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the core gameplay experience, including passwords, government identification numbers, financial account credentials, payment card information, private messages, contacts, or personal photos and videos, unless a future feature explicitly requires such information and appropriate notice is provided.

11. How We Use Information

Information processed through Rush Box and its third-party services may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the games
  • Maintain gameplay progress and statistics
  • Improve gameplay, features, and game balance
  • Understand feature usage and general trends
  • Improve performance and stability
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Deliver and measure advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity
  • Develop new games and features

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product.

12. Third-Party Services

Rush Box currently uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — application analytics and usage measurement.
  • Google AdMob — advertising services.
  • Meta Audience Network — advertising demand through AdMob Mediation.

13. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to the retention practices of the third-party services used by Rush Box and applicable legal requirements.

Locally stored game data generally remains on your device until it is deleted, cleared, reset, or otherwise removed. Analytics and advertising providers may retain information according to their own policies and applicable requirements.

14. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Rush Box. However, no application, transmission method, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of information processed by the application or third-party services.

15. Children's Privacy

Rush Box is designed as a casual gaming application and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

For information about privacy and child-specific practices, please see our separate Child Privacy Policy:

Child Privacy Policy

The application's actual age rating, target-audience settings, advertising configuration, consent implementation, and platform declarations should be configured consistently with applicable requirements.

16. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent where applicable. You may also have rights relating to personalized advertising or certain forms of data processing.

The availability and scope of these rights depend on applicable law. To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the information below.

17. Managing Advertising Preferences

Depending on your platform and location, you may be able to manage advertising preferences through your device settings or privacy and consent controls provided within the application.

Disabling or limiting personalized advertising does not necessarily remove advertisements. You may continue to receive non-personalized advertisements.

18. Device Permissions

Rush Box requests only permissions needed for features enabled in the application. The core games do not require access to contacts, messages, microphone, camera, or personal files unless a future feature explicitly requires such access.

19. Offline Gameplay

The core Rush Box games are designed to support offline gameplay. Certain features, such as advertisement delivery, analytics transmission, remote service updates, or future online functionality, may require an internet connection.

When an internet connection is unavailable, supported gameplay may continue, while analytics events or advertising requests may be delayed, unavailable, or handled according to the relevant SDK's behavior.

20. Rewarded Advertising

Future or enabled versions of Rush Box may offer voluntary rewarded advertisements, such as allowing a player to continue after Game Over. Where available, the reward and required action will be explained before the user chooses to participate. Rewards are subject to confirmation from the advertising provider and availability may vary by region, device, network, and advertising inventory.

21. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new games, features, analytics or advertising services, legal requirements, or changes to our data-processing practices. The Last Updated date will be changed whenever the policy is updated.

Where appropriate and required by law, material changes may also be communicated through the application or another suitable method.
